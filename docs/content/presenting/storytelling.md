> Craft engaging narratives using classic and modern storytelling techniques — Three-Act, Hero's
> Journey, Story Spine, Problem–Agitate–Solve, Situation–Complication–Resolution, in medias res —
> plus hooks, tension, character, pacing, and endings. Use when structuring a technical talk,
> slide deck, blog post, case study, or post-mortem so it reads as a story rather than a list of
> facts.

## What it covers

- **Six core principles** — conflict is the engine, character before concept, show don't tell,
  earn the resolution, one throughline, specificity beats generality.
- **Choosing a structure** — a one-table comparison of seven structures and what each suits.
- **Techniques** — opening hooks, building tension, relatable protagonists, pacing, the explicit
  "so what" moment, and endings that reframe rather than summarize.
- **Common mistakes** and a quick-start checklist.
