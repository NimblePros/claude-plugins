> Add humor to technical presentations — joke placement, comedic timing, humor styles and
> reusable joke formats that land in tech contexts, reading the room, and recovering when a joke
> falls flat. Use when preparing or reviewing a talk, workshop, or slide deck that should include
> jokes, one-liners, visual gags, or lighter moments.

## What it covers

- **Why humor works** — cognitive relief, trust, memory anchoring, and permission to be honest.
- **Humor styles at a glance** — seven styles with their mechanism and reliability.
- **What to avoid** — jokes at the audience's or a named colleague's expense, niche puns,
  punching down, and explaining the joke.
- **Placement and delivery** — where humor belongs in a talk, jokes in notes vs. on slides,
  pausing, and pacing the punchline.
- **Reading the room** — adjustments for developers, executives, learners, international, and
  virtual or recorded audiences.
- **A timing cheatsheet** and a pre-talk checklist.
