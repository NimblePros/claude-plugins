> Write conference talk proposals and session descriptions — titles, abstracts, key takeaways,
> audience level, speaker bios, and private notes for reviewers. Use when responding to a call
> for papers (CFP), writing a meetup, webinar, or workshop description, or turning an existing
> talk into a proposal.

## What it covers

- **What reviewers look for** — audience, takeaways, speaker credibility, fit, and no sales
  pitches.
- **Each part of a proposal** — title patterns and the "is this for me?" test, a
  hook → promise → payoff abstract template, verb-first takeaways (with weak vs. strong
  examples), audience level, speaker bio, and the often-skipped notes for reviewers.
- **A worked example** proposal, common mistakes, and a submission checklist.
