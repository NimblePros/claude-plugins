> Plan and refine multimedia presentations such as slide decks, demos, recorded videos, and
> conference talks, with practical guidance on structure, visuals, rehearsal, and delivery. Use
> when starting, restructuring, or reviewing a talk, webinar, demo, or screencast.

## What it covers

- **The presentation brief** — audience, goal, format, duration, and the outcome you want,
  captured before any content is drafted.
- **Choosing the delivery shape** — slide deck, live demo, recorded video, or a hybrid.
- **Outline first** — hook, problem, approach, evidence, takeaway.
- **Supporting assets and rehearsal** — sparse slides, pre-staged demo data, timed full
  run-throughs on the real setup.
- **A review checklist** for the finished presentation.
